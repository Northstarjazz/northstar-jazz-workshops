How it works

Just show up.

We'll print the books so the charts are there for you. Bring your instrument and play. And if you like to look the tunes over first, they're all shared ahead of time — jam session charts →

The jam is just as welcoming for vocalists as it is for instrumentalists. There's a whole set of vocal tunes ready to go in the charts folder.
