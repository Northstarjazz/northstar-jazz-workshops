Vocal Jazz Ensembles

Sing with a band.

Build repertoire. Grow as a jazz vocalist. Small cohorts of singers working with a live rhythm section — new groups forming now.

Tell us you're interested →

Not a choir — a band, with you out front.

North Star’s Vocal Jazz Ensembles are designed for singers who want more experience working with a live band in a supportive, small-group setting. Each ensemble includes a small cohort of vocalists, a rhythm section, and potentially a horn player or two — the chance to work in a real band setting while still having the support and shared learning that comes from being alongside other vocalists.

This is not a choir or group-singing ensemble. Each vocalist works as a featured singer with the band — bringing in tunes, exploring arrangements, communicating with instrumentalists, and developing their own interpretation of jazz standards. You’ll practice calling tunes, communicating keys and forms, cueing endings, and finding ways to make the music feel personal and alive.

Because there are multiple vocalists in the group, you’ll also learn by listening. One singer may be working through phrasing, another navigating a chart, another experimenting with improvisation. Rather than one vocalist carrying an entire two-hour session, each singer gets meaningful time with the band while also getting to observe and learn from others.

Vocal ensembles meet twice per month for two-hour rehearsals — four hours of band time each month. Tuition is $150/month, and like our other ensembles it’s an ongoing program: members may leave at any time with 30 days’ notice. Once an initial group is formed, we’ll work with members to find a regular meeting time and start date that fits the group.

“You build confidence not by talking about singing with a band — but by doing it, month after month, with supportive musicians, thoughtful coaching, and a group of peers working toward similar goals.”

How it works.

01

Join the interest list

Tell us about your singing background and what you’re looking for. A great next step after Jazz Singer’s Playground, Jazz Voice Lab, or private lessons.

02

Get matched by experience

Our first vocal ensembles are geared toward more advanced vocalists, with each group matched by experience level. More groups will follow as the program grows.

03

Meet your band

A small cohort of vocalists, a rhythm section, and possibly a horn or two. Once the group forms, we’ll find a regular meeting time and start date together.

04

Rehearse twice a month

Two-hour sessions — four hours of band time monthly. Each singer gets meaningful time out front while learning from how everyone else works.

05

Build your book

Repertoire, keys, charts, and arrangements — phrasing, feel, and time. Calling tunes, cueing endings, and leading the band with confidence.

06

Perform & connect

Vocal showcases and community events as the program develops, plus jams, workshops, and member opportunities throughout the year.
