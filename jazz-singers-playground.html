Your way into jazz singing.

The Jazz Singer's Playground is designed for vocalists who want to deepen their connection with jazz standards and build practical vocal jazz skills in a warm, supportive, no-pressure environment. It's our entry point into jazz singing — the place to start before stepping into North Star's other vocal spaces.

Led by vocalist and pianist Tommy Boynton, the workshop moves through the rich tradition of jazz singing — song forms, charts, key choices, and how to communicate with the musicians around you. You'll leave with a real foundation in jazz singing and a clear sense of where to go next in the vocal program. This is the third time North Star has run this class, and it keeps being a favorite starting point.

Course highlights

What you'll explore.

Over six sessions, this workshop builds the practical skills singers need to feel at home in jazz — on the page, in the right key, and in the room with other musicians. It's designed as a foundation you can build on.

Together, you'll:

  • Dive into the rich tradition of jazz singing
  • Understand jazz song forms and structure
  • Learn how to create, read, and communicate jazz charts
  • Improve communication and collaboration with instrumentalists
  • Learn how to choose the right key for each song
  • Explore new standards in different styles
  • Feel comfortable singing jazz repertoire with other musicians

Your Teacher

Tommy Boynton.

Tommy Boynton

Voice & Piano

Tommy Boynton is a jazz singer, composer, arranger, and multi-instrumentalist living in St. Paul. As an artist and educator, he's grateful for the paths that have kept him actively creative and exploring — and he seeks to welcome everyone into experiencing and enjoying music free of judgment and shame. That spirit is exactly what makes the Jazz Singer's Playground the right place to begin.

Tommy is a current member of The Four Freshmen, the jazz vocal group that began in 1948 and has never stopped touring in the years since. He joined in 2015, singing the lead/top part while playing bass, and for the past five years has sung second harmony and played guitar. With the group he's performed at premiere rooms worldwide — the Cotton Club in Tokyo, the Blue Note in Beijing, the Dirty Dog Jazz Café, Live at Emmet's Place, the Oslo Jazz Festival, the Detroit Jazz Fest — and collaborated or recorded with Emmet Cohen, Russell Hall, Kyle Poole, Butch Miles, Ken Peplowski, Katie Thiroux, and more.

Locally, Tommy also teaches at Sarah Jane's Music School and MacPhail. He earned his BM in jazz voice from the New England Conservatory, where he studied with Dominique Eade, Ran Blake, and Fred Hersch. In his spare time he composes song cycles, setting poetry to music — and is an avid cuddler of pets. More about Tommy →
