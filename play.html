Find people to play with.

Jazz ensembles, classes, private lessons, and jam sessions at Jazz Central Studios in Northeast Minneapolis. Whether you’ve been playing for years or are picking your instrument back up after a decade away, we’ll help you find a way in.

  • Coached by experienced Twin Cities jazz musicians.
  • Small groups matched around experience, instrumentation, schedule, and what you want to work on.
  • Opportunities for newer players, experienced improvisers, instrumentalists, and vocalists.

From the community

Programs

All the ways to play.

Ensembles →

Ongoing small groups that meet twice a month, coached by a Twin Cities musician. Learn tunes, work on improvisation, and grow as a band over time.

Group classes →

Focused courses in jazz fundamentals, improvisation, guitar, voice, and more.

Private lessons →

One-on-one instruction shaped around what you want to learn, strengthen, or finally get unstuck on.

Jam sessions →

Free, welcoming jam sessions at Dual Citizen Brewing in Saint Paul. Listen, sit in, and meet other musicians.

Clinics and workshops →

One-time sessions with touring artists, guest teachers, and members of the Twin Cities music community.
