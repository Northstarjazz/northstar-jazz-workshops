The North Star approach

Music as a creative practice.

At North Star, music is more than a skill to improve. It's a creative practice, a form of self-expression, and a way to stay connected — to yourself and to other players.

Across our ensembles, classes, lessons, jams, and workshops, we help musicians make space for that practice in their lives. Founded by working musicians Ben Litwin, Joe Hartnett, and Chris Bates, in partnership with Jazz Central Studios.
