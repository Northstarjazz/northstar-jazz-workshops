Most people who walk in here haven't played with anyone in years.

North Star is a jazz community in Northeast Minneapolis — ensembles, group classes, private lessons and jam sessions for people who want to play with other people. Some of our members are deep in it. Plenty are picking an instrument back up after a decade away, or coming to jazz for the first time after years of something else.

Everything is led by working Twin Cities musicians, and groups are matched by experience level, instrumentation, and what you'd actually like to work on. Ensembles meet twice a month at Jazz Central Studios. Instrumentalists and vocalists both — beginners included.

I recently got my own bass after 10 years of not playing and wanted to get into jazz. Even though I was very nervous, it was the most welcoming environment.
North Star Member

Programs

All the ways to play.

Ensembles

Ongoing small groups that meet twice a month, coached by a Twin Cities jazz musician. Grouped by level, instrument, and goals.

Group classes

Six-session courses like Foundations of Jazz, Improv Lab, and Jazz Guitar Foundations.

Private lessons

One-on-one with working Twin Cities players, matched to your instrument, level, and what you want to work on.

Jam sessions

Second Sunday Jams at Dual Citizen Brewing in Saint Paul. Drop-in, all levels welcome.

Clinics & workshops

One-off sessions with touring artists and guest teachers through the year.
